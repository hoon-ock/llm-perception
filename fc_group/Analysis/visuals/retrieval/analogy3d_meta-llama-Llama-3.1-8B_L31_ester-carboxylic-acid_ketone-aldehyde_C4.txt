NOTES
  The axes carry no tick labels: the PC coordinates are in no unit a reader
  wants off an axis. All three share one scale, so the distances above are
  comparable with each other, and the grid on the panel shows that scale.
  This quadruple is the set's DELIBERATE NEGATIVE CONTROL
  (functional_group_analogy_carbon_matched.py:306-311): the two legs are
  formally matched (H -> CH3) but chemically are not, and it is the one
  that should fail if leg symmetry is what makes an analogy work. It does
  not fail. That is a result, not a plain success.
